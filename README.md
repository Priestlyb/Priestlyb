## What I'm Working On

### Brika

A **design and construction technology platform** focused on transforming architectural plans and building information into more accessible digital experiences.

Brika explores workflows around **floor-plan processing, architectural data, 3D visualization, AI-assisted analysis, and BIM-oriented experiences**, with a focus on making complex building information easier to work with.

### LocalLift

An **AI-powered sales content platform** designed to help businesses create, manage, and grow their marketing content.

Built with React Native, Expo, TypeScript, state management, API integrations, subscription workflows, and a modular feature-oriented architecture.

### Ovena

A **food ordering ecosystem** built around mobile experiences for customers and restaurants.

The platform integrates **food ordering, authentication, payments, delivery workflows, location services, notifications, and third-party services** to provide a connected ordering experience.

## How I Approach Development

I care about more than simply making an application work.

My development process focuses on:

```text
Understand the problem
        ↓
Design the experience
        ↓
Build the architecture
        ↓
Implement the product
        ↓
Test & refine
        ↓
Deploy & iterate
```

I value **clean code, maintainable architecture, good user experience, type safety, and building systems that are capable of growing beyond the MVP stage.**
